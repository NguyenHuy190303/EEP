# Deep search

Choose depth from the unresolved claim and cost of being wrong. Existing evidence can settle a small question; do not open every research channel automatically.

- Current API/library behavior, pricing, versions, benchmarks or a sourced comparison: [research workflow](references/research-workflow.md). Search only applicable channels and prefer official docs/source plus local measurement.
- A substantial new pipeline, training method or system where an existing artifact could change the build: [prior-art workflow](references/prior-art-workflow.md). Check hard fit requirements before popularity or repository quality.

Label evidence T1 official/source, T2 maintainer issue/changelog, T3 community, T0 recollection. Recollection alone is not evidence. State which material claims remain unverified and what was not searched; do not turn a narrow verified answer into an unrelated report. No credentials, internal hostnames or private data in public search queries.
