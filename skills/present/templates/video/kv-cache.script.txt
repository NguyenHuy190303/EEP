A model writes one token at a time.
Each new token looks back at every earlier key and value.
Without a cache, it computes all of them again, every step.
Work grows with the square of the length.
With a cache, it computes one new row, and reuses the rest.
The cost is memory.
For Llama 3.1 8B, 128 kibibytes per token.
At full context, 16 gibibytes. About the size of the weights.
